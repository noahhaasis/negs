Changes to the spektrafilm profiles and LUTs shipped in this folder
====================================================================

The spektrafilm profiles, filter data and LUTs in this folder are by Andrea
Volpato (https://github.com/andreavolpato/spektrafilm) and are licensed under
CC BY-SA 4.0; see SPEKTRAFILM_LICENSE.txt. This file records how Negs' copies
differ from upstream, as that license asks.

2026-10: updated to the upstream experimental branch, commit 28bf883

- profiles/: the colour and B&W profiles are verbatim copies of 28bf883's
  src/spektrafilm/data/profiles. Unchanged.
- filters/: the dichroic, heat-absorbing and lens-transmission data are
  verbatim copies of 28bf883's. Unchanged. neutral_print_filters.json is
  28bf883's with Negs-only entries added (below); upstream's entries are
  unchanged.
- luts/spectral_upsampling/: hanatos2025_irradiance_xy_tc.{npy,toml},
  arctic2026beta04_reflectance_xy_tc.{npy,toml},
  mallett2019_reflectance_xy_tc.{npy,toml} and
  hanatos_irradiance_xy_coeffs_250304.lut are verbatim copies of 28bf883's
  src/spektrafilm/data/luts/spectral_upsampling. Unchanged. Earlier Negs
  versions shipped the hanatos2025 table as irradiance_xy_tc.npy (same bytes)
  and the arctic2026alpha02 tables, which upstream has since removed.

Negs-only data, not from spektrafilm:

- profiles/kodak_trix400_d76_8min.json and
  profiles/ilford_mgrc_deluxe_grade2.json are new preparations from Kodak and
  Ilford datasheets, in spektrafilm's profile format. Sources and method:
  profile-sources/bw-expansion/README.md.
- presets/grain.toml: a kodak_trix400_d76_8min section (rms_granularity 17,
  Kodak F-4017's figure, as Fotufilm's; the rest from upstream's B&W
  defaults), in the Negs-only block at the end of the file. Before it, Tri-X 400 got the schema
  defaults and was about as fine-grained as T-MAX 100.
- filters/neutral_print_filters.json: entries for kodak_trix400_d76_8min on
  every colour paper under TH-KG3, derived with upstream's own solver
  (runtime/print_balance.solve_neutral_filter_shifts at 28bf883) by
  scripts/derive_neutral_print_filters.py, which first re-derives upstream's
  entries for Double-X and three colour negatives to check the method. B&W
  papers (kodak_2302, ilford_mgrc_deluxe_grade2) have no colour to balance,
  so they get no entries and keep the C0 M50 Y50 fallback.

Fotufilm film profiles (CC BY-SA 4.0, by MUAStudio Inc., https://fotufilm.com;
see FOTUFILM_LICENSE.txt):

- profiles/: 24 colour films converted from fotufilm-engine commit 96623ba
  by tools/fotufilm_port (convert.py, port_stocks.py, ship.py). Films Negs
  already had from spektrafilm keep their spektrafilm profiles; the matching
  Fotufilm profiles are not shipped. Changes from the Fotufilm originals:
  sensitivities rebalanced to Negs' neutral-exposure convention; curves
  resampled onto Negs' log-exposure grid and fitted with three norm_cdfs
  sub-layers; slide curves shifted so mid-grey is neutral at Y = 0.18;
  spectral adaptation window fitted per stock (fit_window.py); Fotufilm's
  donor fourth layer (Reala 500D) and per-stock engine features (couplers,
  grain, halation) are not carried over; each profile's antihalation class
  is chosen from Fotufilm's halation strength (CineStill, which has no
  anti-halation layer, gets "no"). LomoChrome, CineStill 400D,
  Lomography Color 100, Phoenix 200, Aerochrome and Infrared Color are marked
  approximate in their metadata, as Fotufilm models them from samples;
  Superia 200 is too, as it renders further from Fotufilm than the others.
  LomoChrome Purple is held back (it renders about a stop darker than
  Fotufilm; cause not yet found).
- profiles/: 5 black-and-white films from the same commit: HP5 Plus,
  FP4 Plus, Delta 3200, T-MAX 100 and Acros 100 II (ilford_hp5_plus,
  ilford_fp4_plus, ilford_delta_3200, kodak_tmax_100, fujifilm_acros_100_ii).
  Changes from the Fotufilm originals: one channel (Fotufilm's three layers
  are identical); sensitivity rebalanced as above; curves resampled and
  fitted with four norm_cdfs sub-layers; the reference curve and Fotufilm's
  development conditions gathered into a development-time family, with the
  datasheet's normal time as default_development_time; base+fog per time
  from each curve's dMin; antihalation "strong", as Negs' Tri-X; target
  print Ilford Multigrade RC grade 2. Fotufilm's B&W paper curve, grain,
  adjacency and halation are not carried over. Each reference curve was
  checked against its manufacturer datasheet graph
  (research/fotufilm/bw-datasheet-check.md). HP5 Plus 9.5/14 min and
  FP4 Plus 6/9 min are Fotufilm's estimates, recorded in their metadata.
- filters/neutral_print_filters.json: TH-KG3 entries for the 15 ported colour
  negatives on every colour paper, from upstream's solver
  (crates/spektrafilm-cli/src/bin/solve_neutral_filters.rs). Existing entries
  unchanged.
- presets/couplers.toml: 11 ported stocks with steep curves get the class
  default DIR-coupler gammas scaled down (factor in each entry's comment), so
  the coupler pre-correction stays well defined (tools/fotufilm_port/fold_check.py).
- presets/grain.toml: grain sections for the 5 B&W films, in a marked
  Negs-only block at the end; upstream's sections unchanged. Each sets
  rms_granularity to the datasheet's RMS granularity (Fotufilm's
  grainStrength x 1000; T-MAX 100 and Acros 100 II checked against the Kodak
  and Fujifilm datasheets) and takes the rest from upstream's B&W defaults.
- presets/grain.toml: grain sections for the 24 colour films, in a second
  Negs-only block. Fotufilm's datasheet figures (Fujifilm still negatives,
  slide films, Aerocolor 2460) are used x 1000, as they match upstream 1:1
  on the films both have; Fotufilm's other figures are scaled by 0.525,
  fitted on Gold 200, UltraMax 400, Ektar 100 and Portra 400; the Fujifilm
  cine films take a fit on Vision3 250D/500T with Fotufilm's layer weights;
  CineStill 800T takes Vision3 500T's section. The rest from upstream's
  colour defaults. Before, every one got the schema defaults.
- borders/35mm/: edge-print strips for the 24 ported colour films (product
  codes or names) and the 5 B&W films (Fotufilm's edge texts),
  crates/spektrafilm-io/src/film35.rs.
