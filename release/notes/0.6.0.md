# Negs 0.6.0

- Dragging a slider is much smoother: while it moves, Negs renders the photo
  at the size it is shown on screen, with every effect on. A frame of a 24 MP
  photo takes about a tenth of a second instead of nearly one. The status line
  says "Preview" meanwhile; letting go renders the full photo exactly as
  before.
- A newly opened photo shows up sooner.
- Halation and DIR couplers render about 1.6× faster on the GPU, with
  identical results.
- Changing Exposure no longer recalibrates the film, which makes each change a
  little faster.
