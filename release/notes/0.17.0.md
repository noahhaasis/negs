# Negs 0.17.0

The Mac app keeps the paper with a lock beside the photograph's name,
gains a dark Plain theme, and prints your own negatives on a new paper as
Auto does.

## Mac

- **Paper lock.** The lock before "printed on", under the photograph,
  keeps the paper when you change film; closed, the label reads "always
  printed on". The paper tray's foot has the same switch, Keep for every
  film. It replaces Settings › Keep paper when changing film and keeps
  what you had set there.
- **Plain dark.** A sixth theme in Settings › Look: Plain in macOS's dark
  greys, light words in the system font and blue marks.
- **Your negative on a new paper.** Choosing a paper, trying one in the
  tray or applying a preset with another paper now balances the frame on
  it as Prep › Auto does, and the paper tiles show it printed that way.
  A frame on a paper always gets the same print, whatever papers and
  filters it had before; changing paper no longer leaves strong casts.
- The window opens at the size and place it was left at.
- A trim mark clicked while the film or paper tray is open starts
  cropping.
- Trying a film or paper no longer shows a rotated photograph unturned
  for a moment.
