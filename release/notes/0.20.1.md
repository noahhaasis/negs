# Negs 0.20.1

A bugfix release for Windows.

- Windows: folders with thousands of photographs no longer make the app
  sluggish or keep a CPU core busy. Since 0.18.0, every thumbnail that
  arrived made the app look through the whole folder once for each
  photograph in the film strip and grid. With 3000 photographs the
  window stopped responding for up to a minute after opening the
  folder, and clicks and scrolling in the grid could take seconds to
  answer. The film strip and grid now find each photograph's file
  directly, take in thumbnails that arrive together at once, after your
  clicks and scrolling, and keep the thumbnail keys they've already
  worked out. While thumbnails are developed in the background, the
  window stays responsive (issue #24).
