# Negs 0.9.1

A bugfix release.

- Mac, Windows and Linux: the inspector no longer jumps while you drag a
  slider. With **Limit preview to 12 MP** off, or a photograph larger than
  the display, the quick preview shown during a drag made the Image Size
  notice appear or change, pushing the sliders below it down until the
  drag finished. The notice now always describes the finished preview.
