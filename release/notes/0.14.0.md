# Negs 0.14.0

A new Prep tab gets your photograph ready before it goes on film, with
tone, colour and detail controls and a match to your camera's brightness.
29 new film stocks join the catalogue, from Velvia 50 and Kodachrome 25 to
HP5 Plus and Aerochrome. Print Curves get a graph you can drag, the film
grid gets search and favourites, and changing film or paper on a large
photograph shows a preview sooner.

## Everywhere

- **Prep.** A new tab before Lab, for the photograph you start from:
  Highlights, Shadows, Contrast, Lift and Gain; Color Boost and Saturation;
  Midtone Detail; and an Exposure control of its own. White balance, lens
  corrections and input colour move here from the Lab. Every control is
  neutral by default, and a neutral Prep leaves the render exactly as
  before. Prep is saved with the photograph and kept when you apply a
  preset.
- **Match camera brightness.** With this switch in Prep on, a RAW is
  developed as bright as its camera's own preview; JPEG, PNG and TIFF files
  are matched to their original pixels. The match follows the look: change
  film, paper, preset, negative exposure or white balance and it's solved
  again. Prep's Exposure slider adds to the match, so your offset survives
  a change of film. Save and Export wait for a pending match. Settings ›
  *Match new photographs to camera brightness* turns it on for new photos
  (on by default in the desktop apps).
- **29 new film stocks**: 24 colour and 5 black-and-white, adapted from
  [Fotufilm](https://fotufilm.com) by MUAStudio Inc. (CC BY-SA 4.0). Each
  has its own grain and edge print. Some are modelled from
  sample photographs rather than datasheets and are marked *approximate*.
  The full list is below.
- **Grain for black-and-white films.** Tri-X 400 had no grain settings of
  its own and was about as fine-grained as T-MAX 100. It now gets its
  datasheet's granularity, so **existing Tri-X 400 edits come out
  grainier**.
- **Film search and favourites.** A search field over the Film grid
  matches every word you type against each film's name and group; Return
  picks the first match, Escape clears it. Star a film or paper to put it
  at the top of the grid. New users start with Gold 200, Portra 400,
  Ektar 100 and Fujicolor C200 starred.
- **Quicker film and paper changes.** On a large photograph, choosing a
  film or paper (or Undo) first shows a preview at the size of the window,
  then the full-size one. Going back to a film you just used is quicker
  too.
- **New photographs start fresh.** Opening a photograph now resets the
  adjustments, keeping your film, paper, Image Size and output colour. To
  carry the previous photograph's edits over, as before, choose Settings ›
  New photographs › *Keep the previous edits*. Relaunching still restores
  your last session with its edits.
- **Post is now Output** and holds Image Size and Border. Its old tone and
  saturation controls are replaced by Prep, which works on the photograph
  before the film rather than on the finished print, so there's no exact
  conversion: **saved sessions with those Post edits open with them reset
  to neutral**. Lab exposure is now called *Negative exposure*.
- The Lab shows each group's basic controls plus anything you've changed;
  *Show all controls* shows the rest.

## Mac, Windows and Linux

- **Print Curves graph.** A graph at the top of Print Curves shows the
  paper's response with your photograph's film exposure behind it. Drag
  the middle handle sideways for Print exposure, the side handles for Slow
  and Fast gamma and the curve for Gamma; double-click a handle to reset
  it. RGB, R, G and B show the neutral curve or one layer.
- **Scopes.** An RGB histogram or RGB waveform above the controls. Drag
  the handle below it to resize it.
- **Film format menu** in Lab › Camera: 110, Half frame, 35 mm, 6×4.5 /
  6×6, 6×7, 6×9 and 4×5, or Custom… for the long-edge slider. Existing
  edits render as before.
- A help tooltip on every control and group.
- Fit no longer enlarges a small photograph past 1:1.
- The window can't be made narrower than its two columns.
- Settings can set a custom viewer background colour.
- *Limit preview to 12 MP* and *Use less memory for export* move to
  Settings. They're now app preferences, outside Undo and Reset.

## Mac

- The export panel's Size field keeps what you type. Before, typing a
  size like 1600 emptied the field on each keystroke and exported at
  2048.
- On macOS versions with Apple RAW 9, Negs tries it first, then Apple
  RAW 8, then its own decoder.

## Windows

- **A redrawn look.** Controls, typography and spacing are redrawn; dark
  mode follows the system without white flashes; Windows 11 gets rounded
  corners. The menus and the window's actions are in the title bar, the
  photograph's tools in a pill on the canvas, and Adjustments | Presets at
  the top of the column. Tab scrolls to the focused control.
- No more flicker switching tabs or entering crop, and no flash of the
  startup photograph while you edit.

## Linux

- Prep, Match camera brightness, the scopes, the Print Curves graph, film
  search, favourites and help tooltips, as on Mac and Windows.
- The AppImage prefers X11/XWayland when it's available, which avoids a
  GTK crash reported on some Wayland desktops. Set `GDK_BACKEND` to choose
  yourself; Wayland-only desktops still use Wayland.

## Web

- [getnegs.com/try](https://getnegs.com/try/) gets Prep | Lab | Output,
  Match camera brightness, film and paper previews, film search and
  favourites, a Settings sheet with an *Advanced controls* option, and
  tooltips on every control. Touch screens work on at most 4.4 MP to stay
  within a phone's memory.

## New film stocks

**Colour negative:** Fujifilm Pro 160NS, Superia 200 *(approximate)* and
Superia Reala 100; Kodak Kodacolor 100 and Aerocolor IV 2460; CineStill
800T and 400D *(approximate)*; Lomography Color 100 *(approximate)*;
Harman Phoenix 200 *(approximate)*; LomoChrome Metropolis and Turquoise
*(approximate)*.

**Slide:** Kodachrome 25 and 200; Fujifilm Velvia 50 and Astia 100F;
Agfachrome 50, 100 and 200.

**Motion picture:** Fujifilm Eterna 500, Eterna Vivid 500, F-64D and
Reala 500D.

**Infrared:** Kodak Aerochrome III 1443 *(approximate)* and Fotufilm
Infrared Color *(creative)*.

**Black-and-white:** Ilford HP5 Plus, FP4 Plus and Delta 3200; Kodak
T-MAX 100; Fujifilm Acros 100 II.
