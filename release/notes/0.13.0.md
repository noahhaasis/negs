# Negs 0.13.0

See every film, paper and preset on your own photograph before you choose
it. Lens corrections arrive on Windows and Linux, and Negs now also runs in
the browser.

## Everywhere

- **Film, paper and preset previews.** Film and Paper are now foldouts
  that open into a grid of thumbnails: your photograph developed with each
  stock, grouped like the old menus. Click a tile to choose that stock.
  Each preset in the Presets list shows your photograph with that preset
  too. Thumbnails are made only while the main preview has nothing else to
  do. On a slower computer, turn off **Film, paper and preset previews** in
  Settings to go back to the simple menus.
- **Keep paper when changing film.** A new setting, off by default, keeps
  the paper and its print development as they are when you choose another
  film. Otherwise Negs still switches to the paper made for that film.
- **Reset a slider by double-clicking it.** Before, you had to
  double-click the slider's title. On Mac and Windows, Option/Alt-click
  also resets a slider.
- **Preflash on the GPU.** Prints with a preflash now come out the same on
  the GPU as in the reference render when the print exposure isn't 1 or the
  scanner's white correction is on. Before, such prints differed from the
  reference by up to about 14 ΔE00 at a print exposure of 0.7.
- More accurate wide blurs on the CPU fallback (32-bit), most visibly with
  wide DIR couplers.
- Moving the enlarger's M/Y shifts or the preflash no longer rebuilds the
  engine's calibration for each preview.

## Mac

- Lens corrections now also work on RAW files that Apple's decoder opens
  but can't correct, such as the Fujifilm X100V's.
- Fixed white balance on Olympus ORF files: every mode except As Shot did
  nothing.
- The photograph from your last session is now in the window as soon as it
  appears, about 0.3 seconds after launch instead of 0.65.

## Windows

- **Lens corrections.** Supported RAW photographs get a **Lens
  corrections** checkbox after RAW White Balance. It corrects distortion
  and vignetting using the data the camera stored in the file (DNG, Sony,
  Fujifilm, OM System/Olympus and Panasonic). It is on for newly opened
  photographs and after Reset. Sessions from older versions keep their
  framing until you turn it on.
- Show Original now follows changes to lens corrections, white balance and
  input colour.

## Linux

- **Lens corrections.** A **Lens corrections** switch in the RAW group,
  for the same cameras and with the same behaviour as on Windows.

## Web

- Negs runs in the browser at [getnegs.com/try](https://getnegs.com/try/).
