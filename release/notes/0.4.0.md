# Negs 0.4.0

- A new Post tab adds brightness, contrast, whites, blacks, and saturation as
  finishing edits on the scan. Lab keeps everything that develops the film;
  the border moves to Post. Post edits redraw without developing the film
  again, and exports match the preview.
- The histogram and the Lab/Post switch stay in place while the sections
  scroll, and Reset Adjustments sits below them. Only Enlarger starts
  expanded.
- The crop's Done button now uses the accent color.
