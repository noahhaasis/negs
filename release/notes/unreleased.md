# Negs (unreleased)

## Mac

- **RAW + JPEG.** If you shoot RAW+JPEG, a RAW and the camera's JPEG
  with the same name (IMG_0412.CR3 and IMG_0412.JPG) are now one
  photo in the strip and grid, marked +JPG. The RAW is the one that
  develops.
  Deleting the photo moves both files to the Trash. Original ⌄ can
  show the camera's JPEG instead of the RAW, so you can hold, split
  or compare side by side against the camera's own film simulation.
  A JPEG you've already rated or edited stays a photo of its own.
  To list every JPEG as before, tick Settings › Photographs ›
  Show camera JPEGs separately.

- **Loading original.** Holding Original, or turning on its split
  line, showed nothing until the original was decoded. For a large
  photograph that can take a few seconds. A "Loading original" tag now
  shows over the photograph until the original appears.

- **Open in Lightroom.** Right-click photographs in the grid or the
  film strip, or choose File › Open in Lightroom (⌥⌘E), to send them
  to Lightroom as full-size 16-bit TIFFs in your export colour space,
  with their EXIF and rating. Lightroom asks once to add them all.
  Negs keeps its copies only until Lightroom has added them, or a
  week at most; Settings › Upkeep can delete them sooner. Shown only
  when Lightroom is installed. With Lightroom Classic, Open in
  Lightroom Classic puts the TIFFs in Pictures › Negs for Lightroom,
  which Negs never empties by itself, and opens Classic's Import window.
  The right-click menu also has Export….
  A batch export started from the grid now shows its progress there.
