# Negs (unreleased)

## Windows

- **The Mac's look.** The window is drawn in the Mac app's themes, chosen
  in View › Theme: Darkroom (the default), Contact sheet, Light table,
  Daylight, Plain and Plain dark. Only Plain follows Windows' light or
  dark mode.
- **A quieter title bar.** One band holds File, Edit, Photo, View and
  Help, the photograph's name in Newsreader, and minimise, maximise and
  close in the theme's colours. Snap layouts still open from the
  maximise button, and Alt, F10 and the underlined letters work as
  before.
- **No toolbar.** Open, Export, the panel and the photograph's tools
  (fit, 100 %, rotate, crop, before and after, the film strip) are in
  the menus, with Ctrl shortcuts. Crop is Ctrl+K or C; Settings is
  Ctrl+,; Reset Edits is in the new Photo menu.
- **A status line in words** under the photograph: its size and how long
  it took to develop, or the export under way with Cancel. Original ⌄
  shows the original while you hold it, or from its menu, and Fit and
  100% zoom.
- **Messages under the title bar.** Errors and questions hang from the
  top of the window over a dimmed view, with the same words and buttons
  as before. Enter answers, Esc cancels, Tab moves between the buttons.
- With nothing open, the window shows where the print will go, "Open a
  photograph" and your recent photographs. Dragging a file over it says
  "drop to open it".
- A photograph that couldn't be developed now says so where it would be,
  with the reason and Try Again, instead of an error box. "Developing"
  and "Updating" show as a small tag at the top of the photograph.
- **The Mac's inspector.** The panel reads like the Mac's. Adjustments ·
  Presets and Histogram · Waveform are words, with Reset on the right.
  Prep, Lab and Output are tabs in Newsreader, each with a count of its
  edits; click the count to see them or reset them. Sliders have a fine
  track and a ring for a knob, ↺ resets one value, and a changed value
  turns bold and underlined in pencil. Rarely used controls fold into
  "N more" lines (View › Show All Controls shows them all), and Output
  ends with the export's size and format above Export Photograph….
  Keys, Tab focus and screen readers work as before.
- **Presets as tiles.** The Presets panel shows each preset as your
  photograph developed through it, with its name, film and paper, two to
  a row; the one you applied last is ringed. Click to apply, drag to
  sort, drop preset files to import them. Each tile's menu has Rename,
  Export…, Show in Folder and Delete…, and the arrow keys, Enter, F2 and
  Del work on the tile with the focus.
- **Settings in its own window.** File › Settings (Ctrl+,) opens a window
  in the app's look with the Mac's tabs: Photographs, Speed, Upkeep and
  Look. Look shows the six themes as small pictures of the window.
  Changes apply at once; Esc closes it.
- Settings › Photographs › Fotufilm films can list all of Fotufilm's
  films, only those made from datasheets, or none. A film already in use
  stays listed.
